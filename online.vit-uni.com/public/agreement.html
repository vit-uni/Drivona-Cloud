2. Data We Collect

2.1 Location Data (Required for Core Functionality)

To provide speed camera alerts, road hazard warnings, and speeding alerts, this App needs to continuously collect and process your precise location data, including:

  • GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude)
  • Vehicle speed information
  • Direction of travel and route
  • Timestamps

Legal Basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — necessary for the performance of a contract (providing the alert service you have subscribed to).

2.2 Community Report Data

When you actively report speed cameras, traffic accidents, road works, or other road hazards via the device button or the App, we carry out distinct processing operations, each based on a single legal basis:

(a) Receiving, validating, and abuse-prevention screening of your report (event type, coordinates, timestamp, associated Device ID) — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the contract), as community reporting is a core feature of the Service you request. Device IDs used for de-duplication and fraud screening are stripped at ingestion and never forwarded.

(b) Aggregating and redistributing event data to the user community — Before any event data is redistributed to other users, we apply the following technical measures to minimise re-identification risk:

(i) Identifier stripping: Device IDs and any other direct identifiers are stripped at ingestion and never forwarded;

(ii) Spatial generalisation: Coordinates are snapped to a coarse geographic grid (currently a [200‑metre / 500‑metre] hexagonal grid), obscuring precise report origin; and

(iii) Temporal rounding:Timestamps are rounded to the nearest [5‑minute] interval, reducing the granularity of any dataset that could be correlated with an individual's movements.

Even with these measures, precise location and time data — even without direct identifiers -present a residual risk of singling out an individual when correlated with other available data. Accordingly, we do not assert that the redistributed event data is irreversibly anonymised, and we treat it as pseudonymised data within the scope of the GDPR.

Legal basis. The processing and redistribution of this pseudonymised event data is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests), supported by a documented Legitimate Interests Assessment ("LIA"). The substance of the LIA is:

  • Purpose: maintaining an accurate, real-time community hazard-alert database that users of the Service reasonably expect;
  • Necessity: real‑time redistribution cannot be achieved by less intrusive means without destroying the core community-alert functionality;
  • Balance: the processing is confined to road‑event metadata, direct identifiers are removed, and spatial/temporal generalisation substantially reduces any residual re-identification risk. Users submit reports voluntarily on an effectively anonymous basis and can control their reporting activity at any time. We have concluded that
  • the interests and fundamental rights of data subjects do not override our legitimate interests in redistributing this data for the benefit of the user community.
  • You have the right to object to this processing at any time (see Section 6). A summary of the LIA may be requested from info@techvsystems.de.

Real‑time metrics and device diagnostic data are generated and accessible locally; access does not require an active internet connection.
